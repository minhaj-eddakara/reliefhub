About ReliefHub System

Natural and man-made disasters often overwhelm traditional relief pipelines. When communications break down and shelters become crowded, delay in aid can cost lives. ReliefHub replaces fragmented paper-based and manual procedures with a centralized, automated disaster response platform.

🤝 Rapid Victim Registration

Victims can quickly self-register and request emergency rescue, temporary shelter, food, or medical aid in seconds.

⚖ Automated Camp Allocation

The intelligent allocation engine automatically balances displaced families into shelters with open space, preventing overcrowding.

📊 End-to-End Transparency

Live request tracking, arrival confirmations, supply logs, and district-wide reports keep all stakeholders synchronized.
